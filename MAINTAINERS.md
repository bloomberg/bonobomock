## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:
- Reviewing and merging pull requests that meet quality and test standards.
- Triaging issues and labeling them appropriately (`bug`, `enhancement`, `good first issue`, etc.).
- Ensuring CI remains green on all supported platforms.
- Preparing release candidates, tagging releases, and updating `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Upholding the project’s [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Maintainers are **not required** to:
- Provide personal support for user issues.
- Review all PRs immediately (but should aim for <7 days for acknowledgment).


## Becoming a Maintainer

Regular contributors can become maintainers after:
1. Submitting at least **5 significant PRs** that have been merged.
2. Demonstrating familiarity with the build, CI, and coding style.
3. Earning trust from at least **two current maintainers** through review participation.

Once approved by consensus, their information is added to this file in a PR.
